# E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce sales data using Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib to identify sales trends, top-performing products, profitable products, and high-performing states.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📊 Analysis Performed
- Data Cleaning
- Product-wise Sales Analysis
- Top Products by Sales
- Product-wise Profit Analysis
- Monthly Sales Trend
- State-wise Sales Analysis
- Business Insights

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### Product-wise
- Laptop is the highest-selling product with approximately ₹9.67M in sales.
- Smartphone ranks second with approximately ₹5.54M in sales.
- Laptop and Smartphone are the major contributors to overall sales.

### Profit-wise
- Laptop generates the highest profit of approximately ₹1.33M.
- Smartphone ranks second with approximately ₹0.68M profit.
- Electronics products are important contributors to profitability.

### Monthly Sales
- July records the highest monthly sales at approximately ₹3.0M.
- October is another strong-performing month with approximately ₹2.8M.
- February records the lowest sales at approximately ₹1.5M.

### State-wise
- Maharashtra is the highest-performing state with approximately ₹5.2M in sales.
- Rajasthan and Uttar Pradesh rank second and third.
- Delhi has the lowest sales among the Top 10 states at approximately ₹1.5M.

## 💡 Business Recommendations
- Focus on high-performing products such as Laptops and Smartphones.
- Maintain sufficient inventory for high-demand products.
- Investigate the reasons behind low February sales.
- Strengthen operations in high-performing states.
- Develop targeted marketing strategies for lower-performing states.
- Monitor product-level profitability regularly.
